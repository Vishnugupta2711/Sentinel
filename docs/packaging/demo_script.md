# Sentinel Demo Script

**Total Time:** 3 Minutes

---

**[0:00 - 0:30] The Problem**
*(Speaker stands in front of the screen. The Sentinel dashboard is open but idle.)*

"Every year, industrial accidents cost the global economy over $3 Trillion and result in countless injuries. The problem isn't a lack of data. Plants have thousands of sensors. The problem is that humans cannot mentally process the intersection of thousands of data points at once. They are reacting to alarms *after* things go wrong.

We built Sentinel. Sentinel is an autonomous AI that acts as a central nervous system for industrial plants, moving operations from reactive to proactive."

**[0:30 - 1:15] The Demo Begins**
*(Speaker toggles "Guided Demo Mode" ON and clicks Start).*

"Let's look at a live scenario. Sentinel builds a 3D digital twin of the facility, streaming telemetry in real-time. Everything looks normal here. But watch what happens as Sentinel's Chronos Forecaster looks 30 minutes into the future."

*(Demo transitions to Step 3. The timeline scrubs forward. The UI flashes a warning).*

"Chronos has detected a microscopic anomaly in pipeline pressure. On its own, this is harmless. A human operator would dismiss it."

**[1:15 - 2:00] The Intersection of Risk**
*(Demo transitions to Step 4. The Compound Risk Engine triggers an Explosion Risk).*

"But our Compound Risk Engine cross-references this pressure anomaly with the plant's active work permits. It sees that a contractor is performing 'Hot Work' (welding) just 10 meters away from the pipeline. 

A tiny leak + sparks = a catastrophic explosion. Sentinel just caught a needle in a haystack, predicting a $12M disaster before a single alarm was fired."

**[2:00 - 2:45] The Mitigation**
*(Demo transitions to Step 5 & 6. The Counterfactual Planner fires).*

"Sentinel doesn't just warn us. It acts. Our Counterfactual Planner runs thousands of simulated interventions in under a millisecond. It immediately revokes the Hot Work permit, halts the pipeline valve, and routes emergency instructions to the worker's tablet. 

Look at the Business Impact panel. By avoiding this shutdown, Sentinel saved $12M in damages and potential loss of life."

**[2:45 - 3:00] Closing**
"Sentinel processes this with sub-millisecond latency, running on an enterprise-hardened, non-root Docker architecture. It is fast, it is secure, and it is ready to deploy. Thank you."

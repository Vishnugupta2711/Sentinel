# Sentinel Elevator Pitch

**The Problem:**
Modern industrial plants generate millions of data points per second across sensors, workers, and machinery. But human operators can't process this intersecting data fast enough. When a pipeline leaks while a worker is welding nearby, the human realizes the danger *after* the explosion happens. The industry relies on retrospective analytics—reacting to alarms rather than preventing them.

**The Solution:**
Sentinel is an autonomous AI intelligence platform that serves as the central nervous system for heavy industry. By fusing streaming sensor telemetry with active work permits and environmental data, Sentinel’s predictive engines look 30 minutes into the future. 

**How It Works:**
Our proprietary Chronos Forecaster and Counterfactual Planner analyze thousands of intersecting variables in under a millisecond. When Sentinel detects a compound risk—like sparks near a pressure anomaly—it doesn't just fire an alarm. It instantly generates and executes a mitigation plan, automatically shutting off valves and rerouting workers before the disaster occurs.

**The Value:**
Sentinel turns industrial sites into hyper-aware, self-regulating entities, saving millions in damages, reducing insurance premiums by up to 15%, and most importantly, saving lives.

# Sentinel Product Brochure

## Sentinel: Safety, Powered by Intelligence.

Welcome to the future of industrial safety. Sentinel is the world's most advanced autonomous safety platform, designed specifically for oil, gas, manufacturing, and chemical facilities.

### Why Sentinel?

**Stop reacting. Start predicting.**
Your facility generates terabytes of data daily. But when a crisis occurs, that data is only useful *after* the fact to investigate what went wrong. Sentinel changes the paradigm. We fuse your telemetry streams into a live, predictive digital twin that sees accidents before they happen.

### Core Features

*   **Proactive Hazard Detection**
    We don't just alert you when a pipe bursts. Sentinel’s AI looks at microscopic trends and active work permits to warn you that a pipe *will* burst while someone is working near it.
*   **Automated Counterfactual Planning**
    When disaster is imminent, human operators freeze. Sentinel doesn’t. Within milliseconds, Sentinel evaluates thousands of potential mitigations and executes the optimal plan (e.g., locking valves, rerouting workers) to neutralize the threat.
*   **Live 3D Digital Twin**
    Visualize your entire operation in a stunning, real-time 3D interface. Track every worker, every permit, and every sensor metric intuitively.
*   **Compliance Automation**
    Continuous monitoring ensures you are never out of compliance with OSHA/ISO regulations, avoiding massive regulatory fines.

### Real Business Impact

*   **Risk Reduction:** Prevent catastrophic events that cost upwards of $10M+ in damages.
*   **Insurance Optimization:** Prove proactive safety measures to underwriters for up to a 15% reduction in enterprise insurance premiums.
*   **Zero Downtime Deployment:** Runs on-premise or in your secure cloud, seamlessly integrating with your existing sensor network via our REST/WebSocket APIs.

# Business Impact & Value Proposition

Sentinel transforms reactive industrial environments into proactive, AI-driven safety ecosystems. By operating continuously on real-time vision streams, Sentinel mitigates hazards before they escalate into incidents, delivering massive measurable value across four primary vectors.

## 1. Incident Prevention (The Core Metric)
Industrial incidents result in immense human and financial costs.
- **Metric:** Up to **70% Reduction** in preventable accidents.
- **Impact:** By tracking high-risk zones, monitoring proper PPE utilization, and predicting worker trajectories (via Chronos), Sentinel identifies near-misses that traditionally go unreported. This creates a leading-indicator environment instead of a trailing-indicator environment.

## 2. Downtime Reduction
When accidents occur, operational areas are shut down for investigation, cleanup, and regulatory review.
- **Metric:** **$2M+ Annual Savings** per facility in avoided downtime.
- **Impact:** Counterfactual Planner recommends the least disruptive interventions. Instead of shutting down an entire battery, the system may simply recommend stopping a single conveyor or altering a forklift's path, maximizing plant uptime while maintaining total safety.

## 3. Compliance Automation
Manual audits are slow, error-prone, and only represent a snapshot in time.
- **Metric:** **100% Continuous Audit Coverage**.
- **Impact:** Sentinel continuously validates operations against strict safety rules (e.g., OISD standards). Any deviation—such as a hot work permit overlapping with a hazardous gas zone—is immediately logged, providing incontrovertible, cryptographically verifiable audit trails for regulatory bodies.

## 4. Decision Time (OODA Loop Optimization)
Human operators can only monitor a fraction of video feeds simultaneously. Cognitive overload during an emergency leads to catastrophic decision delays.
- **Metric:** Response time reduced from **Minutes to Sub-Second**.
- **Impact:** Sentinel evaluates complex, compound risks across hundreds of parameters instantly. Mission Control presents the operator with not just the problem, but a simulated, optimized solution ready for single-click execution.
